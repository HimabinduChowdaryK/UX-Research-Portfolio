Case Study Deep Dive

Travlo: AI Travel Planning Platform UX Research

🎯 Research Objective

Travlo provides AI-generated travel itineraries. However, user feedback indicated hesitations around trust in AI itineraries, complex budget management, and user drop-off before final booking completion. This study set out to uncover why users drop off and how to build confidence in AI suggestions.

🔬 Research Methods Applied

1. Structured User Interviews
2. Moderated Usability Testing
3. Feature Evaluation
4. Qualitative & Thematic Analysis
5. Wireframe Usability Validation

💡 Key Findings & Recommendations

1. AI Transparency & Editability

Users were hesitant to trust static AI itineraries without clear explanations of why specific destinations were recommended. Recommendation: Add modular 'drag-and-drop' itinerary editing and visible confidence tags for choices.

2. Budget Visibility Gaps

Travelers found estimated trip costs ambiguous. Recommendation: Introduce dynamic cost breakdown toggles (flights, stay, activities) directly inside the AI plan builder.

3. Seamless Booking Integration

Fragmented redirect flows for booking caused drop-offs. Recommendation: Streamline one-click partner checkout within the itinerary view.
